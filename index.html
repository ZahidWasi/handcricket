How To Play Hand Cricket

1. The Toss: Call Heads or Tails. The winner chooses whether to Bat or Bowl first.

2. Scoring Runs: Both players pick a number from 0 to 6 simultaneously.

  • If the numbers are DIFFERENT: The batsman scores the runs of their number!
  • If both players pick the SAME number: It's a WICKET (OUT!)

3. The 2nd Innings: The roles swap! The chasing team needs to exceed the 1st innings score before losing their wicket or running out of overs.
